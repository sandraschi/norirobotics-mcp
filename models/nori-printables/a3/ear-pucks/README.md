# Ear pucks

Round covers ("pucks") for the sides of the A3's head. Purely cosmetic — the
robot works fine without them; reprint them in any color or material you like.

**Model:** A3
**Type:** cosmetic
**Rev:** 1
**Files:** `ear-pucks.stl` (print)

## Installation

Each puck mounts with 2× M3×16 mm screws over the speaker holes on the side
of the head. You're welcome to use the same hole spacing to attach anything
you want there — just be aware that covering the speaker holes may muffle
the speaker slightly.
